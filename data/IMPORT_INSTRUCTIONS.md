# How to Import Your Cards

1. Open the app in your browser
2. Go to the **Import/Export** tab
3. Open your spreadsheet in Excel/Google Sheets
4. Select ALL rows (Ctrl+A)
5. Copy (Ctrl+C)
6. Paste into the text box in the app
7. Click **Import Cards**

Your 2,500+ cards will load instantly with notable HOF/star cards auto-flagged.

## Add API Key for AI Features

In Streamlit Cloud dashboard:
1. Click your app
2. Settings > Secrets
3. Add: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY = "your-key-here"`

This enables: Card Scanner, Ask AX chat, Sell Cards listing generator
